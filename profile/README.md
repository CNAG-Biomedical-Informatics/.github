# 🧬 CNAG Biomedical Informatics

**Building trustworthy computational infrastructure for biomedical discovery.**

CNAG Biomedical Informatics is a research software laboratory within
[CNAG's Biomedical Genomics Group](https://www.cnag.eu/teams/genome-research-unit/biomedical-genomics-group).
We develop open-source infrastructure that connects genomic, phenotypic,
clinical, and imaging data with reproducible analysis and trustworthy AI.

![CNAG Biomedical Informatics research software ecosystem](assets/ecosystem.svg)

## 🌐 What We Build

Each project solves a focused problem while contributing to a shared biomedical
research platform. **Project names open their documentation.**

### 🔄 Data Interoperability

- **[Convert-Pheno](https://cnag-biomedical-informatics.github.io/convert-pheno/)**
  interconverts biomedical and phenotypic data standards.
- **[beacon2-cbi-tools](https://cnag-biomedical-informatics.github.io/beacon2-cbi-tools/)**
  validates and ingests data for GA4GH Beacon v2.
- **[OMOP CSV Validator](https://cnag-biomedical-informatics.github.io/omop-csv-validator/)**
  checks OMOP-CDM CSV datasets before ingestion.

### 🧬 Analysis, Metadata & Identifiers

- **[Pheno-Ranker](https://cnag-biomedical-informatics.github.io/pheno-ranker/)**
  compares and ranks interoperable phenotypic profiles.
- **[ClarID-Tools](https://cnag-biomedical-informatics.github.io/clarid-tools/)**
  generates and validates schema-driven biomedical identifiers.

### 🏥 Quality Control & Genomic Workflows

- **[dicomqc](https://cnag-biomedical-informatics.github.io/dicomqc/)**
  audits DICOM metadata de-identification and research-release readiness.
- **[CBIcall](https://cnag-biomedical-informatics.github.io/cbicall/)**
  runs configuration-driven genomic variant-calling workflows.

### 🗄️ Research Data Platforms

- **[Omics Warehouse (OmicsDM)](https://cnag-biomedical-informatics.github.io/omicsdm-documentation/)**
  stores and shares processed omics data with associated pheno-clinical
  information.

### 🚀 Emerging Platform

**Biomedical Research Navigator** is a clinical and assay workspace for
translational research teams, under active development. It brings authorized
datasets, cohort building, phenotype ranking, and curated analyses into a
provenance-aware environment, with policy-controlled access to approved local
or cloud language models.

## 🧭 How We Work

- **Standards first.** Established biomedical standards and transparent data
  models are shared interfaces across the toolkit.
- **Reproducible by default.** Structured configuration and reusable workflows
  make analyses easier to inspect and repeat.
- **Built to last.** We invest in maintainable infrastructure rather than
  isolated prototypes.
- **Open for reuse.** Our software and documentation are developed in the open.

## 🌍 Research Context

Our work grows through the ELIXIR community and European collaborations in
interoperable biomedical data, federated analytics, translational informatics,
and precision medicine, including [3TR](https://3tr-imi.eu/),
[HEREDITARY](https://hereditary-project.eu/), and
[PRECISESADS](https://www.ihi.europa.eu/projects-results/project-factsheets/precisesads).
